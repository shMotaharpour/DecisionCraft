Time granularity — same season, three clocks (42 days, fad decay, elasticity 0.35)

weekly  epochs=  6  |S|=  147  V(s=15)=  145.78  solve   25.3 ms
2-day   epochs= 12  |S|=  273  V(s=15)=  147.11  solve   49.7 ms
daily   epochs= 42  |S|=  903  V(s=15)=  147.31  solve  187.5 ms

value ordering check (must be non-decreasing in fineness):
  weekly 145.78 <= 2-day 147.11 <= daily 147.31  -> OK

read:
 - a finer clock strictly enlarges the policy class -> value can
   only improve; the price is |S| growth (linear here) and more
   decisions to commit to.
 - the weekly plan cannot reprice after a demand shock inside the
   week; the daily plan pays for that option whether or not it
   ever fires (option premium vs information value).
 - rule of thumb: refine the clock until the marginal value of the
   last refinement stops paying for its |S|/|A| cost.
