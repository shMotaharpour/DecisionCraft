Precomputed policy vs MPC — demand shifts 2.0 -> 4.0 at day 15 (promotion)

static VI policy : profit= 220.57   0.00 ms/decision
MPC  (H=5, online lambda) : profit= 230.72   8.50 ms/decision

MPC gain: +10.15 (+4.6%)

read:
 - the static policy was optimal for AVERAGE demand — a model
   that never exists after day 15; it understocks through the
   promotion and pays the whole season for its blindness.
 - MPC with an online estimator rides the shift (no retraining,
   no new policy class — just re-solving the same tiny MDP with
   fresh statistics). Its price: solve time per decision
   (trivial here, the whole point of lesson 1.7 at scale).
 - second MPC cost: myopia. H=5 sees only 5 steps ahead; for
   problems with long lead times the horizon must exceed the
   information delay or MPC quietly becomes greedy.
 - design rule: precompute when the world is stationary and
   decisions are frequent; re-plan when the world moves or the
   model was wrong — and measure the solver budget honestly.
